About Just Ask Pat

Serving people first since 2019.

Just Ask Pat Handyman Services was built around practical help, clear communication and solid workmanship for South Florida homeowners.

A straightforward handyman service

The business was established in 2019 with a simple idea: when something around the house needs attention, homeowners should have someone dependable they can call. That can mean mounting a TV, repairing drywall, changing plumbing fixtures, handling small electrical tasks, improving a bathroom or kitchen, working on irrigation, or tackling a mixed home punch list.

Just Ask Pat is licensed and insured and focuses on serving people with professional work and practical communication. The goal is not to overcomplicate a project. It is to understand what needs to be done and help get it handled.

Service mindset

  • Reliable scheduling and direct communication
  • Practical solutions for real homes
  • Respect for the customer and the property
  • Clean, professional finished work
